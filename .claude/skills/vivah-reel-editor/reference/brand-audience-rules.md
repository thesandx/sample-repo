# Brand, audience, messaging and Sandeep's rules

## Product facts (safe to say)
- vivah.online: a personal wedding website + WhatsApp digital invite for Indian weddings.
- Every guest gets a personal link; it opens with **their own name** on a Shri Ganesh card
  ("You are invited, Hi Neha"), like a paper card addressed to them.
- Tap to open, wedding song on tap, **scratch the gold foil to reveal the date**, live countdown,
  **Google Maps venue**, **all functions listed** (Tilak, Haldi, Mehndi, Sangeet, Reception...),
  "Our Families" section, WhatsApp RSVP, add to calendar, own subdomain (e.g. `priya-rahul.vivah.online`).
- Made from a phone in ~5 minutes in 5 steps: names → date + WhatsApp number → functions →
  address (subdomain) → song. Free preview, then **₹799 one time**.
- Pitched against paper cards (₹5,000–50,000+ for a print run; "aadhe drawer mein pade rehte").

## Claims rules (settled by Sandeep, 24–27 Oct 2026)
- Price **₹799**. Never ₹499 (old price). Never invent other prices for vivah.
- **NEW30** coupon (30% off): use only when Sandeep asks for it in that reel. It's on the v2 FINAL
  reel, the tutorial and 20s demo end cards. The 20s "vivah.online pe jao" ad has **no price and no coupon**.
- No "500+ couples" or any couple/user count, no fake reviews, testimonials, star ratings or
  follower numbers (also Meta ad policy).
- AI Priya **does** act as a customer. Sandeep explicitly overrode the growth plan's rule on this:
  don't re-raise it. But product screens must be real recordings.
- Competitor comparisons only against unnamed "paper cards" / unnamed alternatives.
- Text safe zone: keep text out of the **bottom 20%** (Sandeep's override of the plan's 35%), and
  keep the top ~14% light for stories.
- Music: any track in Instagram's commercial library (Business profile); he adds it himself.

## Audience and tone
- Hindi-speaking brides-to-be aged 20–30 (bride POV), plus "send this to the bestie whose wedding is coming".
- Casual Hinglish chit-chat, like a friend telling a story (e.g. Priya at her mirror putting on a
  bindi). Write scripts as a story: "humne… friends ko bheja… sab pooch rahe the… bas ₹799".
- Goal of every reel: curiosity → visit the site (link in bio).
